Privacy policy

Tabris UG (haftungsbeschränkt) · Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Controller

The controller within the meaning of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for the processing of personal data on this website is:

Tabris UG (haftungsbeschränkt)
represented by its Managing Director Dr. Christina Fritz
Plauener Str. 163-165, Haus D/4, 13053 Berlin, Germany
Email: info@tabris.berlin

2. Key points in brief

  • This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking, analytics or advertising services.
  • No third-party content is loaded. Fonts, images and scripts are served exclusively from our own server.
  • We process personal data only to the extent necessary for the technical operation of the website and for handling your enquiries.

3. Provision of the website and server log files

When you visit this website, your browser transmits technically necessary data to our web server. The following data is recorded in server log files: IP address, date and time of the request, requested address (URL), HTTP status code, amount of data transferred, referrer URL, and browser and operating system information (user agent).

This processing serves to deliver the website, to ensure stability and security, and to defend against and investigate attacks. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in the secure and reliable operation of the website. Log files are deleted after 14 days at the latest unless, by way of exception, they are required for longer to investigate a specific security incident. The data is not combined with other data sources.

The website is operated on a web server within the European Union. Where we use a hosting provider for this purpose, it acts as a processor pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR on the basis of a data processing agreement.

4. Contact form and contact by email

If you contact us via the contact form or by email, we process the data you provide (name, email address, optionally company, subject and content of the message) in order to handle and answer your enquiry. The details from the contact form are transmitted to our mailbox by email; they are not stored in a database of the website.

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR where your enquiry is aimed at initiating or performing a contract, and otherwise Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest lies in answering business enquiries. Providing the data is voluntary; however, without the details marked as required we cannot process your enquiry via the form.

To protect against misuse (spam, automated mass requests), the contact form briefly processes a non-reversible, pseudonymised value (hash) of your IP address in order to limit the number of requests per hour. This value is deleted after 24 hours at the latest. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR.

We delete your enquiries as soon as they have been conclusively dealt with and no statutory retention obligations apply. We retain business correspondence within the retention periods under German commercial and tax law (Section 257 HGB, Section 147 AO).

5. Job applications

If you apply for a position with us, we process your application data exclusively for the purpose of carrying out the application procedure. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR in conjunction with Section 26 of the German Federal Data Protection Act (BDSG). If no employment relationship is established, we delete your documents no later than six months after the application procedure has ended, unless you have consented to longer storage (Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR). Please note that unencrypted emails are not fully protected against access by third parties in transit.

6. Recipients and transfers to third countries

Your data is processed only by those within our company who need it to handle your request. In addition, technical service providers (in particular for hosting and email) receive data exclusively as processors bound by our instructions. Transfers to countries outside the European Union or the European Economic Area are not intended. Data is passed on to other third parties only where we are legally obliged to do so.

7. Cookies, tracking and external content

We do not store any information on your device and do not access information stored there (Section 25 TDDDG). We use neither cookies nor comparable technologies, analytics tools, social media plug-ins, embedded maps or videos. A cookie banner is therefore not required.

Our website contains links to the websites of our brands and companies. Data is transferred to the respective provider only when you click such a link; processing there is governed by the privacy policy of the respective provider.

8. Data security

This website is delivered exclusively via an encrypted connection (TLS/HTTPS). We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, manipulation and unauthorised access.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights:

  • access to your data processed by us (Art. 15 GDPR),
  • rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR),
  • erasure (Art. 17 GDPR) and restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR),
  • data portability (Art. 20 GDPR),
  • withdrawal of consent with effect for the future (Art. 7 (3) GDPR).

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR): Where we process your data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR), you may object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation.

To exercise your rights, simply send a message to info@tabris.berlin.

10. Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The supervisory authority responsible for us is:

Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit
(Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information)
Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany
www.datenschutz-berlin.de

11. Automated decision-making

No automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR takes place.

12. Changes to this privacy policy

We update this privacy policy whenever changes to the website or the legal situation make this necessary. The version published here applies.

This English version is provided for convenience. In case of doubt, the German version prevails.
